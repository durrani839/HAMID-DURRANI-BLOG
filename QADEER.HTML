DR ABDUL QADEER KHAN

**Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan (A.Q. Khan)** was a famous Pakistani nuclear scientist, born on 1 April 1936 in Bhopal, India.
He is widely known for his important role in developing Pakistan's nuclear programme.
He is often called the **Father of Pakistan's Nuclear Programme**.
He played a major role in making Pakistan a nuclear-armed country in 1998.
He died on 10 October 2021 in Islamabad, Pakistan.
