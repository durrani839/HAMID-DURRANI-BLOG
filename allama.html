DR ALLAMA MUHAMMAD IQBAL

Allama Muhammad Iqbal was born on 9 November 1877 in Sialkot
He was a great poet, philosopher and thinker.
He is known as the Poet of the East
He gave the idea of a separate homeland for Muslims of the subcontinent in his Allahabad Address in 1930.
He is also known as the Muffakir-e-Pakistan. He died on 21 April 1938 in Lahore.
