MUHAMMAD ALI JINAH

Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah was the founder of Pakistan. He was born on 25
December 1876 in Karachi. He was a great leader and a famous lawyer.
He worked hard for the rights of Muslims and played an important role in the creation of Pakistan on 14 August 1947.
He became the first Governor-General of Pakistan.
He died on 11 September 1948.
He is known as the Father of the Nation.
